# physical/ — Physical preparation tracking

The SI selection process includes physical events. This folder holds the standards
and your training record.

## Subfolders

| Folder       | Contents                                                                |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `standards/` | Official event standards, per category, each with a `T1_OFFICIAL` source ID |
| `logs/`      | Your own training measurements over time                                  |

## Rules

- **`standards/` is empty and stays empty until an official TGPRB document is
  acquired.** Physical standards are exactly the kind of fact where a wrong number
  causes real harm — training to the wrong distance or time wastes months. No number
  is written here from memory, from a coaching video, or from a model. Official
  document or nothing.
- Until standards are sourced, this area is `BLOCKED`, and it is recorded that way in
  `PROJECT_STATE.md`. That is the honest status, not a gap to be quietly filled.
- Training logs record what was measured, not what was intended: date, event,
  measurement, conditions.
- No medical, dietary, or injury-management advice is generated by this system. That
  is out of scope and belongs with a qualified professional.
